Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data security are fundamental to us. Learn how we handle, store, and safeguard your student information.

GDPR & NDPR Compliant Last Updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

At Scholars Breeding Platform, we prioritize the confidentiality and safety of your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, and protect student data across our WAEC, NECO, GCE, and JAMB prep platforms.

2. Information We Collect

We collect essential information required to deliver personalized academic content, CBT practice tracking, and subscription management:

  • Student Registration Profile: Full name, email address, phone number, and exam preference selections.
  • Academic Progress Data: Test scores, practice time logs, subject performance metrics, and generated Access ID credentials.
  • Transaction History: Records of activated access plans, payment receipts, and subscription statuses.
Payment Security Disclaimer: All monetary transactions are processed via Paystack over encrypted PCI-DSS compliant gateways. Scholars Breeding does not store full debit card details, PINs, or sensitive banking credentials on our infrastructure.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly utilized to enhance your academic experience on our platform:

  • To generate unique student Access IDs and manage platform authentication.
  • To deliver tailored CBT practice questions based on your chosen exam categories.
  • To track study analytics and highlight performance strengths and weaknesses.
  • To issue official transaction notifications and support responses.

4. Data Storage & Infrastructure

We utilize secure cloud environments backed by Firebase Infrastructure to ensure end-to-end data encryption during storage and transit. Automated system backups prevent data loss and safeguard your study records.

5. Your Rights as a Student

As a registered candidate on Scholars Breeding, you reserve full ownership rights over your personal profile data:

  • Right to Access: You can request a summary of your profile data and study analytics.
  • Right to Rectification: Update or correct inaccuracies in your name or exam preferences anytime.
  • Right to Erasure: Request account deletion and associated study data removal upon completion of your exams.
